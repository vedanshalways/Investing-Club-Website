/ 01 — ABOUT

A hands-on education in markets.

The Investing Club isn't a finance lecture. Members run real paper portfolios, pitch ideas to each other every week, and collectively vote on moves for the club's group fund. Everyone learns by doing.

/ 01

Weekly meetings

Thursdays at 3:30pm. Market recap, stock pitches from members, and strategy discussion. Open to all grades — no experience required.

W
/ 02

Paper trading comp

Every member gets a $10,000 virtual portfolio on Quantaify. Top three returns at end of semester take home prizes from the club fund.

Q
/ 03

Group portfolio

We manage a collaborative long-only portfolio. Members pitch, the club votes, officers execute. Every trade is logged and reviewed the following meeting.

G
